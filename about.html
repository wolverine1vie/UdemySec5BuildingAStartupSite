About Mountain Travel

We believe the world's greatest mountains offer some of life's most unforgettable adventures. Discover breathtaking landscapes and experience the beauty of nature with us.

About Us

Mountain Travel organizes exciting mountain adventures for people who love nature and outdoor activities.

Our goal is to help people discover beautiful destinations and create unforgettable memories.

What We Offer

At Mountain Travel, we offer more than just mountain tours. We want every traveller to enjoy an unforgettable experience tailored to their interests, needs, and abilities.

Individual and Custom Tours

Every traveller is different. That's why we can arrange individual tours based on your preferences, schedule, experience, and fitness level.

Outdoor Activities

Discover the mountains through hiking, mountain biking, fishing, and other outdoor activities. Whether you prefer a peaceful day in nature or an active adventure, we help you find the right experience.

Family-Friendly Accommodation

Travelling with children should be enjoyable and stress-free. We help families find suitable accommodation and tours that take children's ages, comfort, and needs into account.

Safety and Local Support

Your safety matters to us. We work with local partners who know their regions and can provide relevant information, guidance, and support. Activities and routes are selected with local conditions and safety considerations in mind.

Trusted Local Partners

We arrange tours only in destinations where we have established local partners. This helps us provide reliable information, coordinate activities, and offer a better experience from arrival to departure.
